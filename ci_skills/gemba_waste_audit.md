name: Gemba Walk 8 Wastes Audit
description: Use when the user describes floor observations, shop floor inefficiencies, clutter, operator fatigue, excess queueing, or requests an 8 Wastes (DOWNTIME) assessment.

Procedure:
1. Frame the Gemba Context:
   - Identify the specific process cell, department, or office value stream being walked.
2. Screen for the 8 Lean Wastes (D.O.W.N.T.I.M.E.):
   - D - Defects: Rework, scrap, incorrect documentation, missing specs.
   - O - Overproduction: Making more or earlier than required by takt time.
   - W - Waiting: Machine downtime, idle operator waiting for parts, batch approval bottlenecks.
   - N - Non-Utilized Talent: Underutilized operator skills, lack of worker empowerment in kaizen.
   - T - Transportation: Excessive forklift travel, unnecessary double handling, moving parts between buildings.
   - I - Inventory: Excess raw materials, excessive WIP accumulation between stations, finished goods buffers.
   - M - Motion: Ergonomic strain, bending, reaching, searching for tools, walking to fetch parts.
   - E - Extra-Processing: Redundant checks, tighter tolerances than customer drawings, duplicate data entry.
3. Quantify the Observations:
   - Estimate time wasted, scrap dollars, or square footage consumed where data is available.
4. Prescribe Lean Countermeasures for Top Wastes:
   - Motion/Transport: 5S workplace organization, Point-of-Use Tooling (POUT), spaghetti diagram redesign.
   - Waiting/Overproduction: Heijunka (level scheduling), Kanban pull signals, One-Piece Flow.
   - Defects: First-piece signoff, andon cord system, visual controls.
5. Format your final response with:
   - GEMBA AUDIT SUMMARY: Focus Area & Key Observation
   - 8 WASTES BREAKDOWN TABLE: [Waste Category | Observed Symptom | Severity (High/Med/Low) | Root Factor]
   - TOP 3 GEMBA KAIZEN ACTIONS: Immediate, practical shop floor countermeasures.
